Project Overview

Building the future of collaborative learning

Our Vision

SkillSwap Hub is a MEAN-stack web platform designed for university students to exchange skills with each other. We believe in the power of peer-to-peer learning and creating collaborative environments.

Our Mission

Students can offer skills they can teach, browse skills offered by others, and send/accept/reject learning session requests. Everyone can both teach and learn from each other.
